Ruby had been riding the train to Longmire for nearly three days and her body ached. It had been forever since she had to be this still for this long, and she wanted nothing more than to walk the rest of the way to Longmire, even if it would more than double the total length of her trip. The fox, whom she had named Toast on account of its coloring, was perfectly content with this situation, and was busy sleeping in her lap, a ball of fluff.

"Next stop: The Royal Station - Longmire. Arriving in 5 minutes." called the intercoms, startling Ruby from dozing off and awakening Toast, who jumped off her lap and, after yawning, started pulling on Ruby's pant leg, in an attempt to get her to pack up.

"Ok Ok hold on bud" she said as she stretched. Out the window the grand city was visible across a harbor. It was an incredibly foggy morning and much of the city was hidden through the mist, but the glowing lights and large buildings were hard to miss.

The car was mostly full, and she had to wait for passengers to give her room before she could get her bag and her sword from the storage area above her. There was a general air of excitement among the passengers - some manner of big event was happening in the city it seemed. It wasn't why Ruby was here, but it sure did make the train ticket more expensive than she would have liked.

The next hour was a blur of leaving the train and getting bags checked and explaining to the city guard that she is allowed to have her sword (she did all the required paperwork, they just didn't recognize her certificate of swordsmanship) and trying to keep Toast calm throughout as well. Everything happened so fast that she felt a bit dazed when she was finally at the other end of it, staring into the endlessly moving city of Longmire from atop the steps of the station.

Unlike places Ruby was familiar with, much of the city buildings were built from the rubble of the old age - large metal containers were often stacked to make homes, or strange vehicles were made into shops, but around the old bones, modern architecture and decoration was everywhere - from large stone arches to mosaics to brightly colored lights from spirit vessels decorating signs.

With Toasts nagging, Ruby regained her senses and grabbed a map from a nearby kiosk. After cross checking with the address on a piece of paper she had in her pocket, she started walking with determination, hoping her ability to navigate it the wild translated at all to traveling in a big city.

The first location Ruby stops at is a post office, where she buys stationary and ink to write to her sister. Then to a hotel to book a room for the night. Finally, she checked the paper again to make sure that she was going to the right place at the right time.

She walked quite a ways before turning down a dark street, then entering a small hole in the wall restaurant.
