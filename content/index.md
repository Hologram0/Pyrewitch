title: What is Pyrewitch
---
The premise is that a mercenary is given a chance to stop the creation of an undead creature called a [[Puppet]]. The main character is named [[Ruby Beywin]] (she/her) and she is a fighter who's recently volunteered for a position in a private/volunteer army founded to protect vulnerable people from being attacked. 

The worlds [[Magic System]] is based on 3 realms, the [[Realm of Spirits]], the [[Realm of the Dead]], and the [[Realm of the Living]]. When a creature in the realm of the living dies, they pass through the realm of spirits, and make it to the realm of the dead.
