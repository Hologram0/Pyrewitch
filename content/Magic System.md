The basis of my magic system are Spirits (see [[Realm of Spirits]]). Spirits are not exactly ghosts, and are more like non corporeal creatures. They have neither lived nor died, and are just some manner of consciousness that exists in the world. Similarly, they have never been, nor ever will be human. 

The inhabitants of the realm of the living can make spirits bodies by creating pieces of artwork. For example, someone could create a toy, or stitch something into fabric or even something larger like a vehicle, and via some ritual they can try to convince a spirit to inhabit that body. Spirits only really inhabit objects that are artistic, and they are free to leave at will, which means people can't abuse them once they have inhabited the object. Spirits are very happy to do tasks, and do anything from public transit to lighting.

There are also godspeakers - essentially someone who has a pact with a god has some abilities granted to them by that god. For example, a godspeaker who communicates with the goddess of life may be able to magically heal wounds, or someone who communicates with the god of the sun may be resistant to fire (the amount of magic the person poses is determined by the god and the pact). The pantheon that has been developed over the years has around 10 gods that are regularly used.

Within the world there are theories that human souls are very similar to spirits, except they can not leave their body at will - the comic more or less confirms this with the creation of puppets, who are human souls that have been forced into a body after death.

However, because the spirit realm is in between the realm of the living and the realm of the dead, any corpse in the realm of the living can essentially create a wall in the spirit realm, (this wall will weaken over time however the strength of the wall can be increased with certain burial rights)

The only way to free a soul from their tether to the realm of the living is to fully destroy the body (either through fire, or through time) a funeral pyre is the best way to do this, although the process is a bit more complicated for puppets, as they also need a route to the realm of the dead to take once the tether is gone.

Your natural death is basically a free "ticket" to the normal afterlife, and the puppets are what happens when that ticket is taken from you.
