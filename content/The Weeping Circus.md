When the Crimson Mask first began her quest to create her hellish realm, she began by offering power in exchange for worshipers. The first man to accept her offer made the first puppet and used it to put on a show. The show was not very popular at first, but with the crimson mask whispering in his ear the man began to create a spectacle. More and more patrons came to see strange human-like creatures fly and dance across the stage, watch them be doused in flames and remain unscathed. The mans greed and corruption grew, and he began to use his circus to find more people to make into puppets, and traveling so no one could catch on. It was the work of a grieving mother (a godspeaker for the God of Death), a reporter, a fairy, and the first of the puppets that revealed the secret of the circus to the world, thus putting it out of business, and leading the arrest of the man via the money the circus made, (he had to be tracked down, since he never lived near the show). Of course, this man was not the only person who was given these powers, other people had done the same, and many chose to pursue other business ventures with it. The cult inside Longmire is the most relevant one, and they are currently using puppets as spies and security, hoping to take control of the city.
