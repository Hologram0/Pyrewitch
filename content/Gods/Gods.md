There are a bunch of deities that fall into the four elemental categories, or the physical manifestations in the world - fire, water, air, earth, and at the time this story takes place there is one major deity ruling each of the four elements

the major pantheon as I've come up with so far are the four elementals (fire, water, earth, air), the three realms ( soldier, poet, king, these three will not be relevant to the story and yes they are directly from the song)  and the rulers of the realms (life, death, and the celestials)

There's a whole lot more underneath the major pantheon, (like a goddess of healing) and they are much more likely to have speakers than the major pantheon

I tend to have a difficult time drawing them (and so therefore don't have many finished drawings available) but they tend to be humanoid, and they all wear some sort of a mask. This is a picture I drew of death, and pretty much the only drawing of one of the gods that turned out properly.
