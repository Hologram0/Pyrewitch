Once in a while, a spirit may choose to inhabit a plant, and will it to grow a body for them, if left undisturbed for the entirety of its growth, the spirit will live inside the body, and leave the plant behind. These creatures are known as fairies, and are most often humanoid/animal shaped creatures made of materials from the parent plant. They can be any size they wish (although larger ones take much longer to grow) and the material determines how long the body lasts. They are very rare and are mostly the stuff of legend and folk tale.
