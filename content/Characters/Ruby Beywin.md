[[A short story about Ruby]]

Ruby comes from a noble family with high levels of arrogance and higher levels of expectations and rules. She grew up with her parents, her younger sister, and the family dog. To Ruby's parents, if the world was cruel then that could justify cruelty - although Ruby is unsure of how extreme her parents would take this sentiment, she knows for a fact that her parents will do anything to ensure the family legacy will remain strong. She was raised as the oldest son in the family and was therefore expected to take over her father's shipping company when her father retired, a company she cared nothing about at any point in her life. When she turned 17, Ruby convinced her parents to send her to school for Swordsmanship. She left and never returned home after that.

This is one of the most important drawings I've done so far on so many different levels, I think the scars on her back are actually going to be related to her work not because she fell out of a tree XD On a technical level this drawing really nails in the art style I want to pursue for my comic. I'm really happy with the colors and the lighting!! I'm not sure how to improve the lineart from here but I really like where I am at. On a character level I think this drawing is the best I've ever done on the difficulties of dysphoria, and just how uncomfortable it can be. She doesn't want to see her own body because it doesn't feel like her own. On a personal level making this drawing and knowing 100% that I was drawing a woman was a very powerful feeling. Separating bodies and gender can be very challenging but at no point did I feel like I was drawing a man with a woman's face - I was drawing Ruby as she is.

Beatrice - Ruby's pet bee?
Beatrice is a mix between a real bee and a mechanical one - the creator used a dead bee as the base and replaced parts with mechanical ones until it could be possessed like any other piece of art. Its a very delicate and expensive contraption used for communications and spying. Ruby's father had been using Beatrice to... keep tabs on Ruby before Ruby realized and claimed Bea for her own. It took some convincing, but Ruby treated Bea like a pet, not a means to an end. Ruby doesn't use Bea's abilities unless she has to.

A fighter by trade, and a novice spirit tinkerer when she has the time, Ruby is a stubborn and strong willed and slightly distractable leader of the gang. While leaving her relatives behind has become much easier now that she's been living in Longmire, she does miss her family's wealth and can occasionally be found splurging when she really shouldn't be spending the money. She's always ready to put others needs before her own, and doesn't mind getting her hands dirty so others don't have to.

Ruby's father made the decisions in the family and held the power both financially and socially, and her mother, by contrast is very soft spoken and stays in line as much as possible. Ruby and her sister were cared for by their mother but she didn't give them much more than that.
