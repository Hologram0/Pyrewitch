They/Them
They are a [[Godspeaker]] for one of the [[gods of life]]
They wear a lot of star motifs because they don't want to be a godspeaker at all and don't want to associate with the goddess of life that has chosen them (specifically the Lamb). Basically they are trying to tell their goddess "even you came from somewhere".

The Godspeakers for the Heron and the Lamb (aka the hunter and gatherer, or the caretakers) are chosen at birth, and found on their third day with a plant of their Caretakers choosing growing around them. Fern grew up to become spiteful of their role as a Godspeaker, and although they use their powers as a Godspeaker to run an apothecary, they’d take any turn they could to irritate their goddess, believing they should have been given a say before being handed such a large responsibility and an equally large target on their back. To Ferns frustration the Lamb don't seem to care, at least not yet.

Fern, her mother, and her grandmother, are all godspeakers for the Lamb. Fern cares deeply for their mother, and resents the Lamb for causing their mother so much grief and pain. Ferns mother is a fantastic cook 🙂‍↕️
